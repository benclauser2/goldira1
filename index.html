What Is a Gold IRA?

A Gold IRA is a self-directed individual retirement account that holds IRS-approved physical precious metals instead of only paper assets.

It is not a separate statutory account type. It is IRA money + metals custody + approved storage.

How a Gold IRA Works

Four steps take retirement money from a 401(k) or IRA to physical metal held under IRA rules.

  1. Open

    Self-directed IRA

    Open a self-directed IRA with an approved custodian.

  2. Fund

    Contribute or roll over

    Fund via contribution, transfer, or rollover (401(k), IRA, etc.).

  3. Buy

    Approved metals

    Choose IRS-approved coins/bars via a metals dealer.

  4. Store

    Approved depository

    Store metals in an approved depository — not at home — to keep IRA status.

Your Gold IRA questions, answered

Quick answers on Gold IRAs, using retirement money, and buying gold with cash.

Is a Gold IRA the same as buying gold ETFs in an IRA?

No. A Gold IRA in this sense holds physical approved metals with custody and depository rules, not only paper gold funds.

Can I store IRA gold at home?

Generally not if you want to keep IRA tax treatment. Approved depositories are the compliant path.

What purity does IRS-approved gold require?

Most bullion must meet high purity thresholds (commonly .995+ for gold bullion), with specific coin exceptions.

What does “Gold IRA money” mean?

Gold IRA money is retirement capital held in a self-directed IRA and used to purchase IRS-approved physical gold through a custodian, dealer, and depository.

How does IRA money move into gold?

Open a self-directed IRA, roll over eligible funds, buy approved metals, and store them in a depository.

Should I cash out my IRA to buy gold?

Usually no — early withdrawals can trigger taxes and penalties. A Gold IRA rollover is the structured alternative.

How do I buy gold with cash?

Choose product type (coins vs bars) and verify spot vs premium, then buy from a reputable dealer.

How is a cash purchase different from a Gold IRA?

A cash purchase uses taxable money, can be stored at home or privately, and gains are taxed on sale. A Gold IRA uses IRA or rollover funds, must be held in an IRS-approved depository, and follows IRA tax rules.

Does gold “make money” like a stock?

Metals can rise or fall in price. The IRA wrapper changes tax treatment and custody rules, not a guaranteed return.
